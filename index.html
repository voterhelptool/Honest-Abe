How Honest Abe Works

Every claim is classified, broken into checkable pieces, and analyzed for language patterns, framing, and manipulation techniques. You get a verdict, a confidence level, and sources labeled by political lean. If a manipulation technique is detected, it's named and explained — not just flagged. If something can't be verified, Abe says why — and gives its best inference rather than going silent.

No account. No data collected. No ads.

Analysis runs through free AI when available. If nothing's reachable, pattern matching runs locally on your device — it always works offline.

Honest Abe is free, open source, and runs on your device.
